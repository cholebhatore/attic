Phase 5 ended with a working GPT: 14 million parameters, learned positional embeddings, dense feed-forward blocks, and a character-level vocabulary. Every frontier model released since 2023 changes at least four of those choices, and each change traces back to a specific cost.

This phase covers the rest of the gap. The first half is what surrounds the model: how text becomes tokens, why the context window is bounded, how a logit vector becomes output, and what happens between pressing Enter and the last streamed chunk. The second half is the architecture itself, ending with three frontier models written out in full.

<Callout type="info">
  ### Every number here is reproducible on paper

Every figure in this module comes from a published `config.json`, a technical report, or arithmetic you can redo by hand. The DeepSeek-V3 parameter count lands on 671,026,412,032, which is the published 671B. The Qwen3-30B-A3B count lands on 30,532,122,624 against a published 30.5B. When a from-scratch implementation reproduces the headline number, the architecture is right.

</Callout>

## Prerequisites

- [Attention Internals](/ai/transformers/attention-internals) for scaled dot-product attention, the `1/sqrt(d_k)` derivation, and cross-attention
- [Masking and Multi-Head](/ai/transformers/masking-and-multihead) for causal masks and head splitting, which GQA generalizes
- [Blocks and Positions](/ai/transformers/blocks-and-positions) for RMSNorm, SwiGLU, residuals, and the RoPE implementation reused throughout this phase
- [Architectures and the Forward Pass](/ai/transformers/architectures) for why decoder-only won and what a forward pass costs
- [GPT from Scratch](/ai/transformers/gpt-from-scratch) for the baseline model every frontier variant modifies
- [Softmax and Logits](/ai/math-foundations/softmax-and-logits) for the log-sum-exp trick that temperature and top-p sampling depend on
- [Information Theory and Loss](/ai/math-foundations/information-the-and-loss) for cross-entropy, which is what the scaling laws fit
- [Devices and Precision](/ai/pytorch/devices-and-precision) for BF16 versus FP16 and why the choice differs between training and serving
- [Normalization](/ai/neural-networks/normalization) for RMSNorm, now applied to queries and keys as well as the residual stream

No new packages beyond the PyTorch and Hugging Face `transformers` from Phase 2.

## What you should be able to do afterwards

- Compute a model's memory footprint at any precision and separate the weight cost from the KV-cache cost
- Explain BPE, WordPiece, and unigram training, and say why a rare word costs more than its meaning suggests
- Derive the crossover length at which attention compute exceeds dense matmul cost, and the KV-cache bytes per token for any config
- Name the failure mode of greedy, beam, top-k, and top-p decoding, and pick a sampler for a given task
- Distinguish a stop token from a stop sequence and say where each one is checked
- Compute the KV-cache reduction from MHA to MQA to GQA to MLA and explain why RoPE must be decoupled in MLA
- Explain top-k expert routing, why routers collapse, and how an auxiliary loss and a gradient-free bias each fix it
- State the Chinchilla rule of thumb, and explain why Llama 3 8B trains on 1,875 tokens per parameter anyway
- Match a parallelism strategy to the thing that does not fit, and name its communication primitive
- Write a Llama 4, DeepSeek-V3, or Qwen 3 block from a `config.json` alone
- Reproduce a published parameter count from a config, and use the mismatch to find the bug
