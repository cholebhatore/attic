Most confusion in auth discussions ("JWT vs Bearer", "OAuth2 vs login", "sessions vs tokens") comes from mixing up questions that sit at different layers. This section separates them first, then builds up from stateful sessions to delegated identity, and ends with working implementations in Node.js and Python.
